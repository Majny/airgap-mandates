What is real, what is simulated

Real

  • The Trezor Safe 3 in my hand: genuine, certificate chain verified
  • Executor on AWS eu-central-1; the agent role is denied tables, KMS, signatures
  • Agent VPC with no route out
  • KMS Ed25519 log, two witnesses cosign every checkpoint, S3 Object Lock
  • Offline check: upstream Go (log signature) + our verifier (entries, cosignatures)

Simulated, labelled

  • Companies, domains, accounts, inbox
  • Treasury approver on the emulator, auto-confirmed (simulated human)
  • Supplier attestation issuers (real crypto, fictitious organisations)
  • VoP responder; the “CFO” voice (Polly)
  • Benchmark humans: a model, not a user study

Every checkpoint is cosigned by two witnesses: W1, and W2 = unmodified upstream litewitness on its own instance (operator simulated; production runs it at a separate organisation). Full table: demo/REAL_VS_SIMULATED.md.

Why a bank buys this

A payment layer an agent can’t talk its way past

  • Mandate for volume, exact-sign for exceptions, attestation network for new payees
  • Every supplier bank that attests its own clients’ accounts makes payee changes safer for every buyer
  • Cost: one hardware wallet per approver · about USD 0.3–0.6 of model compute per 100 agent tasks in our bench
